CAMPUS & MUNICIPAL PARKING & TRANSIT HANDBOOK (PRACTICE EDITION)
Section 1: Visitor & Daily Permits
- Rule VP-01: Daily visitor permits cost $10 per day and are valid from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. in Lot B and Garage 2.
- Rule VP-02: Conference and event guests invited by an internal department receive a prepaid QR code pass. The inviting department pays $8 per pass via internal cost center.
- Rule VP-03: Overnight visitor parking is NOT allowed without a written safety variance approved 48 hours in advance by Parking Enforcement.

Section 2: Shuttle Routes & ADA Headways
- Rule SH-01: The Campus Loop Shuttle runs every 15 minutes from 6:30 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.
- Rule SH-02: Weekend shuttle service operates on Saturday only from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on a 30-minute headway. There is no Sunday shuttle.
- Rule SH-03: All Loop Shuttles are wheelchair-lift equipped. If a lift fails in service, dispatch must send an ADA backup van within 20 minutes.

Section 3: Citation Appeals, Boots & Tows
- Rule AP-01: Drivers have 14 calendar days from the citation date to file an appeal online.
- Rule AP-02: First-time visitor mistakes (such as displaying a valid permit upside down) qualify for a one-time warning if appealed within 14 days.
- Rule AP-03: Lost permit replacements require a $15 replacement fee and a signed loss declaration form.
- Rule AP-04: Vehicles with 3 or more unpaid citations older than 30 days are eligible for immobilization (booting). Fire-lane or ADA-ramp violations are towed immediately with zero warning.

Section 4: Charter Buses & Event Overflow
- Rule EV-01: Events expecting more than 40 guest vehicles must reserve Lot D Overflow at least 5 business days ahead.
- Rule EV-02: Charter buses must stage in the North Bus Apron (max 4 buses at once) and may not idle engines for more than 5 minutes.
